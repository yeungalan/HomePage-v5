# 10 | Studying in Japan #10: Converting a Foreign Driver's Licence in Japan — The Full Process

If you want to keep driving a car or riding a motorcycle while living in Japan, a foreign licence alone is not enough: you have to formally convert it into a Japanese driver's licence. This procedure is called "**外国免許切替**" (gaikoku menkyo kirikae, foreign licence conversion).

Conversions are handled at each prefecture's driving licence centre. Tokyo has three locations: Fuchu, Samezu and Koto. I chose the **Koto Driving License Center (江東運転免許試験場)** in Koto ward.

> <span data-icon="mingcute:file-line"></span> Official reference: [Converting a driver's licence obtained abroad to a Japanese licence (Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department)](https://www.keishicho.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/menkyo/menkyo/kokugai/kokugai05.html)

---

## 1. Is Your Country Exempt from the Tests?

Japan has mutual recognition agreements with **29 countries and regions**. Holders of licences from these places can be exempted from the "knowledge check" (written test) and "skills check" (driving test) and convert to a Japanese licence on documents alone. Exempt places include the United States (certain states), the UK, Germany, France, Australia, Canada and Taiwan.

**Important:** the Koto Driving License Center **only accepts applicants from these 29 exempt countries and regions**. Holders of other countries' licences must go to Fuchu or Samezu and take the knowledge and skills checks.

I hold a US licence, which is exempt, so the whole process was just document checks, fees and an eye test.

---

## 2. Documents Needed

Below are the general documents needed for conversion. Some requirements differ depending on where your licence was issued, so I recommend downloading the official checklists and checking carefully before you go:

- <span data-icon="mingcute:clipboard-line"></span> [Required documents (common) PDF](https://www.keishicho.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/menkyo/menkyo/kokugai/kokugai05.files/hitsuyou071218.pdf)
- <span data-icon="mingcute:earth-line"></span> [Required documents by country PDF](https://www.keishicho.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/menkyo/menkyo/kokugai/kokugai05.files/kunibetuhituyou.pdf)
- <span data-icon="mingcute:warning-line"></span> [Notes PDF](https://www.keishicho.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/menkyo/menkyo/kokugai/kokugai05.files/tyuui.pdf)

The usual documents are:

- **Original foreign licence** (must still be valid; if it does not show the date you first got it, also bring an older licence of the same class from that country)
- **Japanese translation of the foreign licence** (must be issued by JAF or a designated body; you cannot translate it yourself)
- **Proof of a stay of three months or more** (entry/exit stamps in your passport can serve as proof; if your passport has no stamps, you need other documents, such as an employment contract)
- **One 3cm × 2.4cm photo** (stuck on the application form; it is not the photo on the final licence, so any decent photo will do)
- **Residence certificate (住民票)** (for foreign nationals it must show nationality, status of residence and period of stay; if it shows your My Number, cover it or choose not to display it)
- **Original residence card**
- **Original passport**
- **US driving record** (US licence holders need a record showing when they first got their licence, etc.)

If you are applying for both a car and a motorcycle licence, bring the documents for both.

![Checklist of required documents with the "外切" No. 2 entry ticket](https://s3.alanyeung.co/blogphoto/IMG_4806.JPEG)
*The "外切" number ticket I got on arrival, with the official required documents list behind it: go through it once to confirm you have everything*

### About the Japanese Translation

The translation must be issued by JAF (the Japan Automobile Federation) or a body it designates; translations by individuals or ordinary translation agencies are not accepted.

- <span data-icon="mingcute:link-line"></span> [JAF official guide to applying for a translation](https://english.jaf.or.jp/driving-in-japan/drive-in-japan/about-dltas)

![Sample JAF Japanese translation, with fields for name, address, issuing country, licence number and more](https://s3.alanyeung.co/blogphoto/ImagePaste%202026-4-11158058.png)
*A sample JAF Japanese translation: the whole document is stamped and certified by the Japan Automobile Federation, and usually takes two to three days*

I got the translation at a JAF office in Japan; it took only two or three days and was not expensive.

## 3. Booking

Conversions now **must be booked online in advance**; same-day applications are not accepted. The booking system:

- <span data-icon="mingcute:link-line"></span> [Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department online procedures (web booking)](https://www.keishicho-gto.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/keishicho-u/)

Demand is high, and the wait is usually **about two months**. I booked on 12 March, and the earliest free slot was 8:30 a.m. on 11 May, a full two months later.

**Tip:** book as soon as you decide to convert. Do not wait until you have all your documents, or you will wait even longer.

## 5. The Process

### Get a number and go to counter 1

I had booked 8:30 a.m. and arrived at about 8:00. There were not many people, and I got the "外切" (foreign conversion) number 2 ticket.

![Blue number ticket 002 for "1番窓口 外国免許切替"](https://s3.alanyeung.co/blogphoto/IMG_4807.JPEG)
*The blue ticket clearly says "1番窓口 外国免許切替" (counter 1, foreign licence conversion); I was second in line*

Check in at **counter 1** (foreign licence conversion) and tell the staff you have a booking. They confirm it in their records and give you a number to wait with.

![Counter 1, "外国免許証→日本語の切替", with several applicants being served](https://s3.alanyeung.co/blogphoto/IMG_4808.JPEG)
*Counter 1 is where foreign licence conversions are handled; the staff check each document one by one*

### Document check and forms

When your number is called, hand all your documents to the staff, who check each one. Basic Japanese is enough. If you are applying for both a car and a motorcycle licence, you fill in **two application forms**; for a car only, one.

The staff ask when you got your foreign licence and how long you have been driving; just answer truthfully.

![Foreign conversion application form showing "普通免許 初心免除", "大自二" and the fee breakdown](https://s3.alanyeung.co/blogphoto/IMG_4810.JPEG)
*The form shows the fees, vehicle classes and whether you are exempt from beginner status; because I had held my licence for years, I got the "初心免除" exemption and do not need beginner marks when driving*

Once the form is confirmed, the staff do an initial review and ask you to sit down and wait to be called.

### Fees: counter 7

After the review, the staff tell you to go to **counter 7** to pay. Fees vary by what you apply for (Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department official fees, revised October 2025):

- Application fee: standard car ¥2,500, large motorcycle ¥2,800
- Issuance fee: ¥2,350
- Combined-class fee (applying for several classes): ¥200

For a standard car licence plus a large motorcycle licence I paid **¥7,850** in total that day. PayPay and other e-payments are accepted, so you do not need cash.

![PayPay payment records showing two charges to 東京都交通安全協会: ¥4,850 and ¥3,000](https://s3.alanyeung.co/blogphoto/ImagePaste%202026-4-11093622.png)
*PayPay payment records: e-payment is very convenient and saves carrying cash*

Also, because I had been driving for more than a year, I got the **beginner exemption (初心免除)**, so I do not need to put beginner marks on the front and back of the car when driving.

> <span data-icon="mingcute:warning-line"></span> If you have held your motorcycle licence for less than 365 days, the staff will point out a restriction: you can only ride solo, with no passenger on the back, until a full year has passed.

### Eye test: counter 8

After paying, go to **counter 8** for the eye test. It is simple: a machine shows a C-shaped ring and you say which way the gap faces (up, down, left, right), then identify red, yellow and green.

Learning these words is enough: up (うえ), down (した), left (ひだり), right (みぎ), red (あか), yellow (きいろ), green (みどり).

### Health declaration: back to counter 1 for the questionnaire

After the eye test, return to **counter 1**, where the staff give you a "**質問票**" (questionnaire) asking five questions about whether you have had any health conditions affecting driving in the past five years.

![The questionnaire with five health questions, all answered "いいえ" (no)](https://s3.alanyeung.co/blogphoto/IMG_4814.JPEG)
*The questionnaire: fill it in truthfully, add the date and your name, sign and submit*

### Registering the IC card PINs

Next you set two PINs for the licence's IC chip and receive a "**登録カード**" (registration card) recording them. Keep it safe.

![The registration card (IC PIN record) and number ticket 002](https://s3.alanyeung.co/blogphoto/IMG_4816.JPEG)
*The registration card shows the two PIN numbers and a QR code: keep this piece of paper safe*

### Photo: counter 10

Take your application form and registration card to **counter 10** for your photo. **You only get one shot, with no retakes**, so tidy yourself up beforehand.

### Wait on the 4th floor to collect

Once everything is done, take the escalator to the licence issuing area on the **4th floor** and wait.

![Escalator entrance to the 4th floor, with a sign for "免許証交付所・食堂・喫茶ホール"](https://s3.alanyeung.co/blogphoto/IMG_4819.JPEG)
*The 4th floor: the licence issuing area is here, and there is also a cafeteria on the same floor for a meal while you wait*

The 4th floor has an electronic number display. I waited about **30 minutes** before my number was called, which was pretty smooth.

![The 4th-floor waiting area, with electronic boards showing numbers for different services](https://s3.alanyeung.co/blogphoto/IMG_4826.JPEG)
*The 4th-floor waiting area: the electronic boards show numbers for several services at once; just watch for yours*

While you wait, the cafeteria on the same floor is reasonably priced: curry rice ¥650, tonkatsu set meal ¥860, a rare bargain.

![Food models at the 4th-floor cafeteria, including curry rice, katsu curry and tsukemen](https://s3.alanyeung.co/blogphoto/IMG_4821.JPEG)
*The 4th-floor cafeteria menu: lunch while waiting for your licence fits the timing nicely*

### Check the IC chip data and collect

When your number is called, an IC chip reader nearby shows the dates you obtained the standard car and large motorcycle licences. Once you confirm they are correct, collect your licence.

![IC chip confirmation screen showing "普通取得年月日 令和08年" and "大自二取得年月日 令和08年"](https://s3.alanyeung.co/blogphoto/IMG_4828.JPEG)
*The IC chip confirmation screen: after confirming both dates are correct, go to the counter to collect your licence*

## 6. What Does the Converted Japanese Licence Look Like?

After conversion you get a proper Japanese IC driver's licence (運転免許証), identical in appearance to licences issued to Japanese residents.

![Sample Japanese driver's licence showing name, address, issue date, validity period, classes and more](https://s3.alanyeung.co/blogphoto/ImagePaste%202026-4-11174946.png)
*A sample Japanese driver's licence: a licence obtained through foreign conversion has exactly the same format as a local one, valid from the issue date until one month after your third birthday*

A few things to note:
- **Domicile (nationality)** is not shown on the front of the licence; it is stored on the IC chip
- **Date of birth** is shown in the Japanese era calendar; Reiwa 08 is 2026
- **Validity** runs from the issue date until one month after your third birthday
- **Beginner mark (初心者マーク)**: if you are a first-time licence holder, you must display beginner marks on the front and back of your vehicle for the first year; if you received the beginner exemption, you are exempt

---

## Summary

The whole foreign licence conversion, from booking to finishing on the day, took about two months. The actual time at the centre on the day was about **two to three hours**. The process is clear and orderly, and manageable even if your Japanese is not very fluent.

Key points:

- **Book early**; the wait is usually about two months
- Holders of licences from **the US and the other 29 exempt places** do not need to take tests and can convert directly at the Koto centre
- Get the **JAF translation** in advance; do not leave it to the last minute
- You can pay with **PayPay** on the day, so no need for lots of cash
- With over a year of driving experience you can get the **beginner exemption**
- Route: Tozai Line, Toyocho Station **Exit 3**, 5-minute walk
