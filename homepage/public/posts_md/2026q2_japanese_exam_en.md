# Grammar Summary

## ～ています

Expresses:

- Doing something right now
- An ongoing state

### Examples

- 勉強しています。
- 雨が降っています。

---

## ～ていました

Expresses that you were doing something in the past.

### Examples

- 昨日、勉強していました。

---

## ～たことがあります

Expresses that you have done something before.

### Examples

- 日本へ行ったことがあります。

---

## ～てもいいです

Expresses that something is allowed.

### Examples

- 入ってもいいですか。

---

## ～なければなりません

Expresses that you must do something.

### Examples

- 勉強しなければなりません。

# Easily Confused Expressions

## くれる vs もらう

### くれる

Someone else gives to me.

- 友だちがプレゼントをくれました。

### もらう

I receive.

- 友だちにプレゼントをもらいました。

---

## 教える vs 習う

### 教える

To teach someone.

- 日本語を教えます。

### 習う

To learn from someone.

- 日本語を習っています。

---

## 聞く vs 聞こえる

### 聞く

To actively listen / to ask.

- 音楽を聞きます。

### 聞こえる

To be audible, to hear naturally.

- 外の音が聞こえます。

---

## 行く vs 来る

### 行く

To go.

- 学校へ行きます。

### 来る

To come.

- 友だちが来ます。

---

## 住む vs 泊まる

### 住む

To live somewhere.

- 東京に住んでいます。

### 泊まる

To stay overnight.

- ホテルに泊まりました。

## Core Idea

Japanese number readings split into "common readings", "less common readings" and the small-っ sound changes caused by pronunciation rules. First, the basic common readings:

- 0: ゼロ / れい
- 1: いち
- 2: に
- 3: さん
- 4: よん
- 5: ご
- 6: ろく
- 7: なな
- 8: はち
- 9: きゅう
- 10: じゅう
- Reading 0: ゼロ vs れい

## The number "0" has two common readings:

ゼロ comes from the English "zero", while れい comes from the Chinese 零. Both are used a lot in daily life, but in slightly different situations.

Phone numbers: either works. For example, "03-1020-XXXX" can be read "ゼロさん…" or "れいさん…".
Time: usually れい. For example, 深夜0時15分 is read 深夜れいじ15分.
Age: either works. For example, 0歳 can be ゼロ歳 or れい歳.

## Reading 4: よん vs よ

The common reading of 4 is よん, but before some counters the ん is dropped and it is read just よ. This is mainly for smoother pronunciation.

4円 (4 yen)
Read よえん, not よんえん.
4年生 (fourth grade)
Read よねんせい, not よんねんせい.

## Additional Notes

Besides the common readings, some readings are used only in specific cases, and some sounds change when words join.

Less common readings

Some numbers are read differently in particular words. These readings are relatively rare and mostly appear in fixed expressions.

- 0: まる. まる means "circle". When reading strings of digits (such as room numbers or building names), まる is sometimes used instead of ゼロ or れい.
101ビル (101 Building)
Read いちまるいちビル.
306号室 (Room 306)
Read さんまるろくごうしつ.
- 4: し. し sounds the same as 死 (death), so it is rarely used in everyday counting. It mainly appears in fixed words such as months.
4月 (April)
Read しがつ.
- 7: しち. Mainly used in fixed expressions such as times and months.
7月 (July)
Read しちがつ.
7時 (7 o'clock)
Read しちじ.
- 9: く. く sounds the same as 苦 (suffering), so it is rarely used. Mainly used in fixed expressions such as times and months.
9月 (September)
Read くがつ.
9時 (9 o'clock)
Read くじ.

## Sokuonbin and Handakuonbin (sound changes)

When 1, 6, 8 or 10 is followed by a counter starting with a sound from the か, さ, た or は rows, the end of the number becomes the small っ for easier pronunciation. This comes from the "entering tone" of classical Chinese and is called 促音便 in Japanese.

- 1 (いち) → いっ
- 6 (ろく) → ろっ
- 8 (はち) → はっ
- 10 (じゅう) → じゅっ / じっ
In addition, when the counter starts with a は-row sound, besides the small っ there is also 半濁音便: the は-row sound becomes a ぱ-row sound.

Some common examples:

- 1
一回 (once): いっかい
一冊 (one volume): いっさつ
一点 (one point): いってん
一杯 (one cup): いっぱい
- 6
六回 (six times): ろっかい
六杯 (six cups): ろっぱい
- 8
八回 (eight times): はっかい
八杯 (eight cups): はっぱい
- 10
十回 (ten times): じっかい / じゅっかい
十杯 (ten cups): じっぱい / じゅっぱい
