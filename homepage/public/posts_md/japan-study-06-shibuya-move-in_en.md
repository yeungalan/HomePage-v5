# 06 | Studying in Japan #6: Moving to Shibuya — A Full Record of the Move-in Procedure

Soon after my language school classes officially began, I decided to move to Shibuya ward and start the next chapter of my life in Tokyo. After filing my move-out at Toshima, the next step was to register my move-in at the **Shibuya ward office**.

**Shibuya City Office (渋谷区役所)**
Address: 〒150-8010 1-1 Udagawacho, Shibuya-ku, Tokyo
Phone: 03-3463-1211　|　Hours: Monday to Friday 8:30–17:00

## What Stands Out at the Shibuya Ward Office: Highly Digitised

Compared with the Toshima ward office, the biggest difference at Shibuya is that its administrative processes are **highly digitised**, with almost no need to fill in stacks of paper forms by hand.

At the Shibuya ward office (near Shibuya Station), I told the counter I wanted to register a move-in, and the staff gave me a number ticket and told me to take a seat and wait. I barely had to fill in anything myself: when my turn came, I handed over the **move-out certificate** issued by Toshima and my **residence card**, and the staff pulled up my details on the computer, generated a fully pre-filled application form, and asked me to confirm that my new address and other details were correct.

As in Toshima, the Shibuya staff also brought out a map to carefully confirm which building and room I was moving into, to make sure the address was registered precisely.

## Registering My Seal at the Same Time

While doing the move-in, I asked to do **seal registration** at the same time, which saved me a separate trip. Seal registration is just as simple: fill in a form and stamp your seal to leave a record.

## Updating the Address on the Residence Card

Once everything was done, the staff handed back the following documents all at once at another counter:

- **Residence card** (with the new Shibuya address on the back)
- **Residence certificate (住民票)** (if you applied for one)
- **Seal registration certificate**

When you get your residence card back, **be sure to check on the spot that the address on the back is correct**. If there is any mistake, ask the staff to fix it immediately; correcting it after you leave is more of a hassle.

## Continuing Health Insurance

Notably, Shibuya **issues a new health insurance card immediately** when you register your move-in, so your coverage is not interrupted by the move. This differs from some other wards where you have to wait for it by post, and is very convenient if you need to use your health insurance right away.

## Summary

The whole process of moving out of Toshima and into Shibuya covered move-in registration, seal registration, a residence certificate and updating the address on my residence card. Because the Shibuya ward office is more digitised, it went much more smoothly than I expected. Remember to apply for seal registration and a residence certificate at the same time, so you can finish several procedures in one go and avoid repeat trips later.

With the address on my residence card officially updated to Shibuya, a new chapter of life began.
